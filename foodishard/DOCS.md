# FoodIsHard

Open **Dinners** from the Home Assistant sidebar.

- **Week:** tap *Plan my week*, then swap (↻) anything you don't fancy or lock (🔒) the ones you want to keep. Use ⋯ for leftovers, eating out, or to choose a recipe yourself.
- **Shopping:** tick items as they go in the trolley. The ⌂ button marks something you already have. Use *Shop* grouping for click & collect, and *Copy*, *Download* or *Print / PDF* to take the list elsewhere.
- **Settings:** set portion sizes, the baby's eating stage, which day your week starts on, and your weekly staples.

Data is stored in `/data/state.json` and included in Home Assistant backups.
