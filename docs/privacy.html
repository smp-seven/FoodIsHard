FoodIsHard

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

FoodIsHard is a free, not-for-profit project, run by an individual (the host) for people they invite. The host is responsible for your information. This policy explains what FoodIsHard stores, why, how long for, and your rights.

Contacting the host: use Report a problem in the app (Settings → Help), or ask the person who invited you or your household to pass your message on.

What is stored

  • About you: your name and email address (so you can sign in), the name of your household, who invited you and when, when you last used the app (recorded at most once an hour, so the host can see which places are unused), whether you've dismissed the latest announcement (so it only shows once), and each time you agreed to the terms of use (and which version).
  • About your household: meal plans, shopping lists, prices you enter, settings such as portion sizes, any allergies you choose to record, recipe ratings and notes, and, if you turn on Life is hard, your household routines, with the time and name of each of the last few ticks.
  • Allergies are health information. They're only stored if someone in your household ticks them, after being asked to confirm. Only tick allergies for the people you cook for, with their agreement.
  • Little ones, only if you add them: whatever first name or nickname you give them (it's optional), the month and year they were born, and how many weeks early, if they were. Never their exact date of birth.
  • Problem reports you send: what you write, your household's name and your first name (so the host can follow up), the app version, your device type and browser, and the last error the app hit.
  • Never stored: passwords (there aren't any) or payment details.

Who can see what

  • People in your household share its plans, lists, settings and ratings, and can see each other's name, email address and when they last used the app.
  • The host sees recipe ratings and notes without your name or household attached, only the day they were left, so they can improve the recipes; and problem reports, as described above. Your household's other data isn't shown to the host in the app. It's kept in a database in the host's Cloudflare account, so as the account owner the host could access it, but only would if you asked, for example to help fix a problem.
  • Other households can never see your data. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for advertising.

Why, and the legal basis

UK data protection law asks us to say which legal basis we rely on for each use:

  • Running the app you've joined (your account, signing in, keeping your household's plans and lists in sync between devices, showing the right recipes): this is needed to provide the service under the terms of use you agreed to (contract).
  • Allergies: your explicit consent, given when you tick the first one. You can withdraw it at any time by unticking them.
  • Recipe ratings, problem reports, when you last used the app, announcements and the record of agreeing to the terms: the host's legitimate interests in improving the recipes, fixing problems, managing the limited number of places, and keeping a record of agreement. These are kept to the minimum needed.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the online version, stores its data, handles sign-in and sends sign-in codes. Household data is stored in Cloudflare's EU data centres. Like any website, pages and sign-in pass through Cloudflare's worldwide network on the way to you. Cloudflare is a US company. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google, only if you choose "Sign in with Google": Google confirms your name and email address to the sign-in service. FoodIsHard doesn't receive or ask for access to anything else in your Google account. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Once a week the app downloads the UK food price index from the Office for National Statistics. That request contains no personal data.

How long it's kept

  • Shopping lists: deleted automatically after 8 weeks.
  • Meal plans: deleted automatically after a year.
  • Settings (including allergies and little ones) and recipe ratings: until you change them or your household is deleted.
  • Life is hard routines: until you remove them. Each keeps only its last 6 ticks; older ones are deleted as new ones come in.
  • Problem reports: until the host deletes them, once the problem has been dealt with.
  • A count of when ratings and reports were left (used to show the host how many are new): 400 days.
  • Your name, email, last-used time and record of agreeing to the terms: until you delete your account or are removed.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how it's used, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent (for allergies, by unticking them). Many of these you can do yourself:

  • Download a copy of your household's data at any time from Settings → Your data.
  • Change your settings, allergies and little ones' details in Settings, and change your own display name.
  • Delete your account yourself, any time, in Settings → Your account. If you're the last person in your household, the household and all of its data are deleted too; otherwise your name is taken off its past ticks and notes.
  • Members of a household can remove each other.
  • For anything else, contact the host as above.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, please tell the host first (see "Contacting the host" above), and they'll respond as soon as they can. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

FoodIsHard is for adults aged 18 or over. Parents can optionally add their babies and young children to plan their portions; any details about them are entered by the parents and kept to a minimum, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. If the change is significant, you'll be told in the app.
