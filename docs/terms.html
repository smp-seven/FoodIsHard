Food safety: please read

  • The recipes, nutrition figures and prices are general guidance only. They haven't been checked by a dietitian, doctor or other professional.
  • Allergies: the allergy settings only hide recipes that list an allergen. They can't know what's in the products you buy, and products change. Always check the label, every time. If you or someone you cook for has a serious allergy or medical condition, take professional advice.
  • Vegetarian: some cheeses and other products are made with animal rennet or gelatine. Check for the vegetarian symbol.
  • Babies: baby notes are general ideas, not medical advice. Follow NHS weaning guidance and your health visitor, prepare food to a safe size and texture, avoid whole nuts and added salt, and never leave a baby alone while eating.
