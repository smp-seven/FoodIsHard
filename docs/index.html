FoodIsHard

Dinner, sorted.

FoodIsHard is a small, free meal planner for vegetarian households. It plans a week of dinners, gives simple recipes (with optional baby-friendly portions), and builds a shared shopping list split by supermarket.

It's a personal, not-for-profit project, not a company. At the moment it's invite-only: there's no sign-up page, and you can only use it if the person who runs it invites you.

Signing in

If you've been invited, open the link you were sent and sign in with the email address you were invited with, either with Google or with a one-time code sent by email. There are no passwords.

Before using it, please read the privacy policy and the terms of use, especially the food safety notes.
